WEGO Simultaneous Turns, Auto-Engagement & Multi-Action Movement

WEGO Turn Structure (4 AP / Player)

  • • Planning Phase: P1 queues secret orders (4 AP), passes device via privacy screen, then P2 queues secret orders (4 AP).
  • • Multi-Action Chained Movement: Any unit may chain multiple 1 AP move legs in a single turn (up to 4 AP). Road bonus (+1" Land) applies per leg.
  • • Step 1 — Movement & Resupply: All units animate leg-by-leg simultaneously. Spent units touching or passing a friendly Base/Resupply Hub (0.62") along any leg reload immediately!
  • • Step 2 — Automatic Engagement (0 AP): All Loaded units automatically fire at the closest valid enemy in range. Mutual kills possible. Firing makes a unit SPENT.
  • • Step 3 — Missiles: Missiles (Atk 8) strike; Air Defense within 3" intercepts with D4.
  • • Step 4 — Logistics: Idle Resupply ticks (2 turns), production spawns, 4 AP construction/repairs complete, and +1 Missile restocks every 5 turns.

Action Point Costs & Field Hubs

  • • Automatic Engagement: 0 AP (Automatic, no manual attack button).
  • • Move Unit / Queue Missile: 1 AP each.
  • • Produce Unit: 2 AP (facility enters 2/3/4-turn cooldown).
  • • Construct / Convert / Repair: 4 AP (entire turn).
  • • Infantry → Resupply Hub: A Secured Infantry (held position 1 turn) can spend 4 AP to convert into a permanent Resupply Hub (Pot, HP 3). Canceled if destroyed in Steps 2–3!
  • • Victory: Destroy both enemy Original Main Bases simultaneously.
