WEGO Simultaneous Turns, Ordered Engagement, Multi-Action & Custom Maps

WEGO Turn Structure

  • • Planning Phase: Each player starts with 4 AP; maximum AP increases by 1 every three turns. Future orders remain private until their turn.
  • • Online auto-pass: Turn on Auto-pass to lock when your opponent locks. When both players enable it, both lock automatically each turn. The AP warning asks for confirmation if any current-turn AP remains; switch it off for unattended play.
  • • Online review: After resolution, each player is ready automatically after 10 seconds. Replay is available during review and finishes before that player advances.
  • • Free Movement: Each unit may move once per turn for 0 AP. Road bonus (+1" Land) applies to that move; route planning schedules longer routes over multiple turns.
  • • Groups: Drag or Shift-click to select units, save named groups for the current campaign, and plot a group route. Units move together at the slowest unit's speed and keep their formation. Removing a group move removes the remaining group route.
  • • Production queue: Each facility holds up to five orders. Use Fill beside a unit type to queue that type into the remaining slots.
  • • Secondary bases: Each player may have four major bases, minor bases, ports, and air bases in total. New secondary bases must be at least 3" from every main base.
  • • Rally points: Set a destination on a production facility; newly produced units spawn normally and automatically receive a route on their next planning turn. Routes must be traversable for the unit.
  • • Scaling: By turn 15 units gain up to +2 attack-die sides and 20% movement speed; every five units beyond three adds another attack-die side. Production cooldown falls by one turn from turn 8 (minimum one).
  • • Step 1 — Movement & Resupply: All units move simultaneously. Spent units reload on contact with a compatible resupply source. Drone launches occur after movement.
  • • Step 2 — Standing Engagement (0 AP): Engage stays active until canceled. Loaded units ordered to engage fire at the closest valid enemy in range after movement; a drone launch temporarily takes priority. A loaded unit attacked without an order returns fire if it can attack an enemy targeting it. Units fire once; mutual kills are possible. Firing makes a unit SPENT.
  • • Fog of war: Enemy units are visible within friendly engagement range or 3" of a friendly structure; drones, building stealth, and submarine sonar have special rules below. Structures remain visible.
  • • Step 3 — Missiles: Global D8 strikes affect both sides within a 1.5" blast, destroy buildings, and cannot damage Main Bases. Air Defense within 3" intercepts with D4; Corvettes protect ships within sonar range.
  • • Step 4 — Logistics: Idle Resupply ticks (2 turns), production spawns, 4 AP construction/repairs complete, and +1 Missile restocks every 5 turns.

Action Point Costs & 4× Scale Map Rules

  • • 4× Battlefield Scale (68"×44"): Default map is 68"×44" and custom maps scale to a 68" longest edge. Outskirts deployment zone is 6.0".
  • • Buildings (B): Land units cannot cross Building (B) tiles except snipers; aircraft fly over them. Artillery destroys building tiles in its blast area.
  • • Pan & Zoom (1×–8×): Scroll wheel zooms centered on cursor; hold Space+Drag, Middle-Click, or toggle ✋ Pan to navigate.
  • • Engage / Move: 0 AP. Artillery barrage / Missile: 1 AP. Produce: 2 AP. Construct / Hub / Repair: 4 AP.
  • • Victory: Destroy both enemy Original Main Bases simultaneously.

Unit Shape Spd HP Atk Range CD Rules

Road Bonus: Land units starting on or moving along a marked highway or custom Road (R) receive +1" speed per leg.

Specialist units

  • • Artillery spends 1 AP to barrage 1"–3" away, but cannot move and barrage in the same turn. One attack roll hits both sides within 0.75"; buildings in the blast collapse and kill snipers inside.
  • • Snipers conceal themselves in building tiles until firing; entering another building tile conceals them again. Drones reveal them within 2".
  • • Bridgelayers spend 2 AP to place a d4 pontoon bridge from their final position to land across water within 2". Land units can cross it on later turns. Land units spend 2 AP to place a destroyable roadblock up to 2" long: choose the first endpoint within 0.62" of the unit and then the second endpoint. It blocks all land units on both sides.
  • • Ammo convoys carry three charges and restock once per turn at a friendly base. They reload nearby spent allies; an enemy in contact steals one charge per convoy per turn, storing it for its next shot if already loaded. Bombers cannot use convoy ammunition.
  • • Drone operators spend 1 AP to deploy a drone within 5" after movement. It reveals units within 2" for this turn and the next; operators reload at logistics sources, not by waiting.
  • • Carriers produce aircraft and reload nearby aircraft. Strategic bombers rearm only at an Air Base or Carrier. Corvettes detect submarines and intercept missiles aimed at ships within 2". Submarines start submerged and become submerged again after a move turn without firing.
