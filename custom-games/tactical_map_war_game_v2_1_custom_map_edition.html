WEGO Simultaneous Turns, Ordered Engagement, Multi-Action & Custom Maps

WEGO Turn Structure (4 AP / Player)

  • • Planning Phase: P1 queues secret orders (4 AP), passes device via privacy screen, then P2 queues secret orders (4 AP).
  • • Multi-Action Chained Movement: Any unit may chain multiple 1 AP move legs in a single turn (up to 4 AP). Road bonus (+1" Land) applies per leg.
  • • Step 1 — Movement & Resupply: All units animate leg-by-leg simultaneously. Spent units touching or passing a friendly Base/Resupply Hub (0.62") along any leg reload immediately!
  • • Step 2 — Ordered Engagement (0 AP): Loaded units ordered to engage fire at the closest valid enemy in range after movement. A loaded unit attacked without an order returns fire if it can attack an enemy targeting it. Units fire once; mutual kills are possible. Firing makes a unit SPENT.
  • • Fog of war: Enemy units are visible only within a friendly unit's engagement range or 3" of a friendly structure. Structures remain visible. Hot-seat players can review the resolved turn from either perspective.
  • • Step 3 — Missiles: Missiles (Atk 8) strike; Air Defense within 3" intercepts with D4.
  • • Step 4 — Logistics: Idle Resupply ticks (2 turns), production spawns, 4 AP construction/repairs complete, and +1 Missile restocks every 5 turns.

Action Point Costs & 4× Scale Map Rules

  • • 4× Battlefield Scale (68"×44"): Default map is 68"×44" and custom maps scale to a 68" longest edge. Outskirts deployment zone is 6.0". Unit speeds (1"–4") and engagement ranges (1"–3") remain at original inch values.
  • • Impassable Buildings (B): Land units cannot move onto or cut through Building (B) tiles, and structures cannot be built on them. Route around city blocks via Ground (G) and Road (R) corridors. Planes fly freely over buildings.
  • • Pan & Zoom (1×–8×): Scroll wheel zooms centered on cursor; hold Space+Drag, Middle-Click, or toggle ✋ Pan to navigate.
  • • Engage order: 0 AP. Move / Missile: 1 AP. Produce: 2 AP. Construct / Hub / Repair: 4 AP.
  • • Victory: Destroy both enemy Original Main Bases simultaneously.
